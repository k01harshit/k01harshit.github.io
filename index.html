About

Research depth, engineering discipline.

Profile

I take problems from whiteboard to paper to production, and I care about models that are fast, label-efficient and grounded in theory.

My research spans neuromorphic learning and graph machine learning. I co-authored SADP, a linear-time alternative to STDP published in Neural Networks. In graph learning, I build rewiring and self-supervised methods for heterophilic graphs and hypergraphs.

On the applied side, I built newspaper segmentation software for the Kerala Government, a multimodal image-to-song recommender presented at IEEE ICRM 2025, and worked on pharmacovigilance with LLMs at IIT Patna.
